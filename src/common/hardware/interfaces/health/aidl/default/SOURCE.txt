HAL: android.hardware.health
Upstream: device/google/cuttlefish/guest/hals/health/health-aidl.cpp
Tag: android-16.0.0_r4 (thirdparty/reference/cuttlefish)

CVD implements IHealth by subclassing hardware/interfaces/health/aidl/default
Health and faking a charging battery (UpdateHealthInfo). This NDK port keeps
that fake-battery policy and registers BnHealth v3 (GSI FCM on this tree;
CVD's xml advertises v4 / getHingeInfo).

libhealthd / HalHealthLoop are not linked; binder registration matches the
rest of this project's NDK HALs.

IStorage lives in health/storage/cvd/ (CVD Storage.cpp).
